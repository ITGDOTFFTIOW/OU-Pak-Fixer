OPEN UNLEASHED PAK FIXER - FAQ AND TROUBLESHOOTING
==================================================

Full guide with screenshots:
https://github.com/ITGDOTFFTIOW/OU-Pak-Fixer/blob/main/GUIDE.md


What do Good, Broken and Clash mean?
------------------------------------
  Good   - ready to go.
  Broken - the pak needs fixing (bad texture headers, or a name that loads
           before the game's own files, such as one starting with $).
           Tick it and click Fix selected.
  Clash  - another installed pak replaces the same item. Only one shows in
           game. Remove the one you don't want.


A pak has several skins in it. Which one do I get?
--------------------------------------------------
All of them. A .pak that contains several skins puts every one of those
skins in the game. Pick each bike and livery in game to see them.


Two installed paks replace the same skin. Which one shows?
----------------------------------------------------------
That is a Clash. Both paks replace the same skin slot, so the game can only
show one of them, and it uses whichever one loads first. To choose, keep the
one you want installed and Remove the other.


The Showroom shows a pak as several cards. Did it split the pak?
----------------------------------------------------------------
No. The Showroom shows everything in a pak as separate cards so it is easy
to look through. It does not split those files up in the game. The pak is
installed as one file, and everything in it goes into the game together.


Can I build my own pack from the skins I pick?
----------------------------------------------
Custom Packs are on the way. They will let you build your own pack from the
skins you choose.


How do I find my game folder?
-----------------------------
The app finds it by itself. If it picks the wrong one, click Change on the
top bar and choose the game's data folder (the one with common.pak).
For Steam that is usually:
  C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\MX vs. ATV Unleashed\data
In Steam you can also right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files
and open data.


A skin doesn't show up in game.
-------------------------------
- Check its status in PACK MANAGER > Installed. Broken paks must be fixed
  first.
- If it says Clash, another pak replaces the same item. Remove the other one.
- Make sure you picked the right bike and team/livery in game. The Showroom
  card shows which bike and livery the skin replaces.
- Restart the game after installing or removing paks.


Windows blocks the .bat or PowerShell.
--------------------------------------
- Right-click the downloaded zip > Properties > tick Unblock > OK, then
  extract it again.
- If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info >
  Run anyway.
- The .bat files start PowerShell with -ExecutionPolicy Bypass for this app
  only. Your system setting is not changed.


How do I undo or uninstall?
---------------------------
- To take a pak out of the game: tick it under Installed and click Remove.
  It moves to Pack Files\Disabled, and nothing is deleted.
- To restore an original download: the untouched copy is in
  Pack Files\Originals.
- To uninstall the app: remove your paks from the game first if you want the
  game back to stock, then delete the OU Pak Fixer folder. The app never
  changes the game's own paks or your save.


Where can I get more skins?
---------------------------
The MVA Unleashed Mod Collection on ModDB:
https://www.moddb.com/mods/mva-unleashed-mod-collection
Put downloaded paks in Pack Files\Broken and fix them from
PACK MANAGER > Broken.


The window app won't start.
---------------------------
FIX PAKS.bat and Pack Manager\MANAGE PACKS.bat do the same fixing and
turning paks on and off from a console window. See the Fallback section of
the guide:
https://github.com/ITGDOTFFTIOW/OU-Pak-Fixer/blob/main/GUIDE.md#fallback-fix-paksbat-and-manage-packsbat
